Delete your account

Lifter Log – Workout TrackerUpdated November 5, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and data from Lifter Log – Workout Tracker (lifterLog). You can do it in the app, or email us if you no longer have the app. You don't need to reinstall it.

Delete it in the app

Open the Profile tab and tap Delete account. If the app is showing the subscription screen, open Account & data there and tap Delete account. If your account has a password, you'll be asked to confirm it. Your account is erased right away.

Delete it by email

Email support@lifterlog.app from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll check that the request is yours and delete the account within 30 days.

If you can't send from that address, for example because you hid your email when signing in with Apple, say so in your message and we'll find another way to confirm the account is yours.

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings as well.

What is erased

Your account, training data, body scans and measurements, preferences, and subscription record. The original body scan files you uploaded (InBody and DEXA photos and PDFs) are removed from our storage at the same time.

What is kept

  • Store billing records: kept by Apple and Google under their own policies.
  • Posts, replies and votes on the feature requests & issues board: these stay on the board, unlinked from you and without any identifier. You can delete your own posts and replies from their menu before deleting your account.
  • Usage data at our analytics and crash-reporting providers (PostHog and Sentry): kept for those services' retention periods. Email us if you want the analytics data linked to your account deleted.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Delete some data and keep your account

In the app, you can delete:

  • any single body scan or measurement;
  • all of your scans at once (Analytics → Body → ⋯ → Delete all scans);
  • any of your board posts or replies, from its menu.

For anything else, email support@lifterlog.app from the address on your account and tell us what you want deleted. We'll do it within 30 days.
